The Project

Project Overview

Zayed Lagoons is a premium residential compound developed by Roshdy Developments and marketed by NBIG in one of Egypt's most prestigious locations — Sheikh Zayed City. The compound blends contemporary architecture with natural water features, lagoons, and lush landscaping to create a truly unique living experience.

  • Stunning lagoon and water feature designs throughout the compound
  • Modern residential apartments with premium finishes — G+5 typical buildings
  • State-of-the-art facilities including pools, gym, and clubhouse
  • Developed by Roshdy Developments · Marketed by NBIG
